# Character and Pokémon sprites

The people and Pokémon that stand next to each landmark, plus the docks and
fishermen on the water, are cropped from two community sources.

## Sources

| Folder | Source | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `pret/` | [pret/pokeemerald](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald) event-object sprites (`graphics/object_events/pics`) | Prof. Abedul, Aura, Brendan, Wally (fallback), Phoebe, Drake, fishermen, sailors, Devon employees, the Lavacalda hot-springs woman, Maxie, Archie, and the characteristic Pokémon (Wingull, Zigzagoon, Kecleon, Azumarill, Poochyena) |
| `showdown/` | [Pokémon Showdown](https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/) trainer sprites | The eight Hoenn gym leaders, the Elite Four, the champion and Steven/Wally |

`frontend/public/game/people/<key>.png` contains the front-facing, trimmed
frame used by the runner. The raw downloads stay in `pret/` and `showdown/` for
reproducibility.
